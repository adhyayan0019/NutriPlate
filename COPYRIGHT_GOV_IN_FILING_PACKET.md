## 2. Description of the Work (Statement of Particulars - Col 9 & 10)

Paste this exact paragraph in the **Description of Work** box:

> *"The work titled 'NutriPlate-Food35' (NP-Food35) is an original electronic database and compilation comprising 7,351 purified, high-variance annotated Indian culinary vision records across 35 distinct taxonomic categories (6,959 training images, 392 validation images, total size 1.28 GB). Developed at the Department of Computer Engineering, Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE), Pune jointly with equal intellectual contributions by student researchers Adhyayan Goyal, Aditya Chavan, Likhit Chaudhary, and Vaishnavi Bighot under the faculty guidance of Dr. Kannan Rajeswari, the compilation embodies substantive human and algorithmic creativity. It systematically resolves historical blind spots in computational dietary assessment by formulating dedicated class heads for foundational Indian thali components (including Yellow Dal, Dal Makhani, Jeera Rice, Steamed Rice, Onion Salad, and Raita). The dataset incorporates an automated watermark purification pipeline that eliminated 290 corrupted stock artifacts, coupled with a synthetic domain-randomization engine that algorithmically injects Gaussian lens defocus blur (sigma 1.8 to 3.5), directional mobile camera motion-shake, and ambient dining illuminance modulation to train deep neural networks robust against real-world smartphone capture distortion. Protected under Section 2(o) of the Indian Copyright Act, 1957 as an Original Literary Work (Electronic Compilation & Database)."*
