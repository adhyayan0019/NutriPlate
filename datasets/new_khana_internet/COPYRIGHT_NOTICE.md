## 3. Substantive Intellectual & Creative Contributions (*Modicum of Creativity*)
Under the established jurisprudence of the Supreme Court of India (*Eastern Book Company v. D.B. Modak*), copyright subsists by virtue of distinct skill, judgment, and algorithmic intervention:

1. **Independent Taxonomy Formulation:** Systematic elimination of computer vision blind spots by curating primary classes for foundational Indian thali components (`dal`, `dal makhani`, `jeera rice`, `steamed rice`, `onion salad`, `raita`).
2. **Automated Watermark Inpainting & Cleaning:** Algorithmic removal of border watermarks, website URLs, and photographer stamps via 6.5% margin trims and Navier-Stokes inpainting, combined with the permanent purging of 290 corrupted stock images to produce **7,351 pure raw food images**.
3. **Synthetic Domain Randomization Pipeline:** Automated injection of mobile-camera artifact models:
   * Gaussian lens defocus blur ($\sigma \in [1.8, 3.5]$)
   * Directional camera motion-shake
   * Real-world ambient dining illumination reduction ($0.55\text{--}0.78$)
